## Team
TM1: Chris Groves

## Ghost Grub
The assembly object is a sandwich created inside a food truck. The player assembles sandwiches by grabbing individual ingredients and placing them in the correct assembly area in the truck.

Uses lighthearted spooky theme, with skeleton customers who each have their own order. Each sandwich begins and ends with a piece of bread with numerous ingredients to add such as bacon, cheese, chicken, lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion, egg, and/or an additional bread. 

Ingredients are selected using the VR Controllers. Once a sandwich has been assembled the player can pick up the completed sandwich and launch it toward the customer using either Ray Button/Trigger button. Completed sandwiches can also be picked back up and thrown again if the player misses. Incorrect ingredients or sandwiches can be discarded using the trash can. 

Customers enter the food truck area through a simple queue system.

### What you'll find
- VR grab interactions for individual sandwich ingredients
- Sequential sandwich assembly
- Visual placement guidance for ingredients
- Multiple sandwich ingredient types
- Completed sandwiches that can be picked up and thrown
- Re-grabbing and re-throwing completed sandwiches
- Trash disposal for unwanted ingredients and sandwiches
- Customer order validation
- Correct and incorrect customer reactions
- Customer animations and sound effects
- Point-to-point customer movement
- A three-customer queue system
- Dynamic sandwich order display
- A stylized spooky food truck environment
